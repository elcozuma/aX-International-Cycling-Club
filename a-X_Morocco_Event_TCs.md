## 2.0 Event Structure

**2.1** Current Event: a-X Anti-Atlas Group Expedition.

**2.2** Location: Southern Morocco / Anti-Atlas Region.

**2.3** Event Dates: 22 March 2027 – 28 March 2027.

## 3.0 Eligibility

**3.1** Participants must:
- Be at least 18 years old
- Be physically capable of completing the expedition
- Have sufficient self-sufficiency and remote-riding competence
- Use appropriate equipment
- Wear a helmet at all times while riding
- Carry and use appropriate front/rear lighting and visibility equipment where conditions reasonably require it
- Confirm they hold suitable insurance

**3.2** Participants are solely responsible for ensuring that they hold a valid passport and any visa, entry permission, immigration documentation or other authorisation required to enter Morocco and remain in the country for the full duration of the Expedition. a-X is not responsible for any inability to participate arising from a participant's failure to meet applicable entry or immigration requirements.

**3.3** a-X reserves the right to reject or remove participants before or during an event at its sole discretion.
